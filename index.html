Our approach

More than a search firm.

Since 2021, Blake has built BJSK around a simple belief: the right hire only works inside the right team. That is why every engagement starts with the people doing the hiring, not with a CV database.

We speak directly with hiring managers to understand what they really need, from the skills on paper to the dynamics of the team. Then we draw on a network of over 1,000 high-quality candidates from tier 1 firms to find the person who fits.

Where it helps, we go further: advising on team design and helping restructure teams so new talent can make an impact from day one.
